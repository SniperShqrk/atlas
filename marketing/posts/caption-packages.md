# ATLAS — Post Captions & Hashtags (Batch 1)

Six pieces, paired with the files in this folder. Positioning throughout follows the marketing plan: lead with the diagnosis (what the app tells you), not "AI workout planner" — every fitness app claims that now.

One real constraint before you post any of this: there's no live link yet (no TestFlight, no hosted landing page URL — the landing page HTML exists but isn't published anywhere). So none of these push "link in bio" toward a broken destination. They're all "building in public" plays — the job right now is audience and proof-of-concept, not installs. Once TestFlight exists, swap the end-card CTA line for the real link.

**Platform notes:** Instagram feed (static) or first slide of a carousel if you want to pair it with `post-balance-card.png` as slide 2. On TikTok, repost as a photo-mode post with the caption as on-screen text — photo posts with a strong claim in the first frame perform fine there too.
